Contact support

For questions, bug reports, or feedback, email th3m4rty@gmail.com.

When reporting an issue, please include the Gearfolio version, your device model, iOS version, and a brief description of what happened. Please do not send confidential client details or financial data unless necessary.

Useful tips

  • Gearfolio's core equipment and project features are designed to work offline.
  • If cloud backup or sync is enabled, check your Apple Account and network connection when troubleshooting.
  • Gearfolio Pro purchase and restore options depend on product availability and your App Store account.
