GEARFOLIO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Gearfolio is an iOS app for organizing photography equipment, planning shoots, managing project workflows, and preparing project documents. This policy describes how information is handled in the app and through optional third-party services.

Support

1. Contact

For questions about this policy or privacy requests, contact the Gearfolio developer at th3m4rty@gmail.com.

2. Data you add to Gearfolio

Depending on how you use the app, Gearfolio may store equipment details, photographs of gear, project names and descriptions, shoot locations and dates, approved gear selections, packing checklists, optional project financial values, and information you enter when preparing client proposals or photographer briefs.

This content is used to provide the functions you request. Project financial values and documents are not intentionally sent to advertising providers as advertising content or targeting parameters.

3. Local storage and optional iCloud

Gearfolio is designed to work offline. Your project and equipment information is stored on your device. If you enable iCloud synchronization or backup in the app, eligible app data and associated files may also be stored and transferred using Apple's CloudKit service in your private iCloud database.

You can disable cloud synchronization and backup in the app without intentionally deleting your locally stored content. Cloud-stored information is subject to Apple's service terms and privacy practices. Existing cloud copies are not necessarily deleted merely because you turn off synchronization.

Learn more: Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Photos, documents, and sharing

Gearfolio accesses photographs you choose to import or attach to equipment and projects. If you generate a PDF, it is created locally on your device. You decide whether to save, print, or share that PDF through iOS. Sharing a document with another person or service is controlled by the destination you select.

Client proposals can include personal contact details, terms, and pricing that you deliberately enter or choose to include. Check the document before sharing it.

5. Advertising and consent

Gearfolio includes integration with Google AdMob for banner advertising in the free experience. Ad serving may be disabled during testing or until required consent and configuration steps are completed. When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, and other data described in their policies, subject to applicable settings and consent.

Where required, Gearfolio uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage advertising privacy choices. You can review available privacy options through the app when that control is offered. Apple tracking permission, if requested, is handled separately through iOS settings.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Help.

6. Gearfolio Pro purchases

If Gearfolio Pro subscriptions become available, purchases, subscription status, and billing are processed using Apple's App Store and StoreKit services. The developer does not need your payment card details to provide the subscription features. Purchase availability may vary while the service is being prepared.

7. Retention and deletion

Content stored locally remains until you remove it in the app, delete the app or its data, or take other actions that remove it from your device. Cloud copies, pending sync changes, and files saved separately in the Files app may follow different retention rules.

For questions about deleting or accessing your information, email th3m4rty@gmail.com. To manage data stored in your personal iCloud account, you may also need to use Apple's iCloud and device controls. Deleting the app from a device does not necessarily remove previously created iCloud records or exported documents.

8. Security

Gearfolio is designed to keep your content in local device storage and, when enabled, your private CloudKit database. No software or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Please protect access to your devices and Apple Account.

9. International use and your rights

Gearfolio may be available in multiple countries. Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise control personal information. Contact us at the address above for requests concerning information processed by the developer. Requests relating to data held by Apple or Google may also need to be submitted directly to those providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Gearfolio's features, service providers, or data practices change. The updated version and its date will appear on this page.
